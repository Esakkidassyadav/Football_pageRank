Who Runs the Team?

Player Influence Ranking in Football using PageRank on Passing Networks. Every completed pass is a link from one player to another, like a link between two web pages. PageRank, the method Google first used to rank web pages, finds the players the team's passing runs through.

How the check works. There is no "correct answer" for who is the most influential player, so PageRank is compared with the closest thing we have: the official Player of the Match of all 115 World Cup 2022 and Euro 2024 matches. For every match the award winner's team is ranked by each method, and we count how often the winner comes out on top. The whole-match network is used here, because many winners were substitutes or were substituted.

What is in the data

Where it comes from

StatsBomb Open Data: free JSON files on GitHub (github.com/statsbomb/open-data). StatsBomb records every action of a match: who passed, who received the ball, where on the pitch and at what minute. StatsBomb ask anyone who publishes or shares work based on this data to name StatsBomb as the source and use their logo.

Player of the Match: World Cup 2022 from Goal.com (a fan vote on FIFA+), Euro 2024 from UEFA.com (chosen by UEFA's technical observers).

No personal data: only professional match events and the players' names from match reports.

How the project works, step by step

  1. Collect. data_prep.py downloads the match list, the events and the line-ups of every match (about 650 MB of JSON) from StatsBomb's GitHub page.
  2. Clean. Only completed passes are kept: a pass that did not reach a team-mate links nobody. Penalty shoot-outs are dropped. Set pieces are marked, so they can be switched off. Each club gets one name for the season (StatsBomb's files mix "ATK Mohun Bagan" with "Mohun Bagan Super Giant", and "Hyderabad" with "SC Delhi").
  3. Build the network. One network per team per match. Players are the nodes. A pass from A to B is a directed link, weighted by how many passes went from A to B (A → B is not the same as B → A).
  4. Choose the time window. Either the starting XI until the team's first substitution (the usual choice for passing networks) or the whole match with every player who came on.
  5. Rank. PageRank is written by hand in pagerank_engine.py (power iteration) and checked against the networkx library on all 460 team networks. Passes made, passes received and betweenness are worked out too.
  6. Show. Passing networks on a pitch, rankings, a tournament leaderboard and charts on this website.
  7. Evaluate. evaluate.py compares PageRank with the official Player of the Match for all 115 World Cup 2022 and Euro 2024 matches.

Data: StatsBomb Open Data (free event data from StatsBomb). Player of the Match: Goal.com (World Cup 2022) and UEFA.com (Euro 2024). PageRank: Brin and Page (1998). For education only.
